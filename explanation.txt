💡 Li-Fi Project Explanation — English & Malayalam

Project title: Li-Fi — Light Fidelity: Arduino-Based Wireless Communication Through Light

1. English explanation

Good morning respected judges, teachers, and my dear friends.

Today, I am presenting my electronics project called Li-Fi, which stands for Light Fidelity.

We use Wi-Fi to connect our devices wirelessly. But did you know that light can also be used to transmit information? Li-Fi is a communication technology that uses modulated light to transfer data.

My project consists of two Arduino Uno boards, an LED, and an LDR light sensor module.

The first Arduino acts as the transmitter. When I type a message on the computer, the Arduino converts the characters into digital bits. The LED switches ON and OFF according to those bits, transmitting the message through light.

At the receiving end, the LDR detects the changes in light intensity. The second Arduino reads these changes, decodes the digital bits, and displays the received message on the computer's Serial Monitor.

The main advantage demonstrated by this project is that information can travel through light without a physical wire connecting the transmitter and receiver.

Li-Fi technology has potential applications in smart buildings, selected hospital environments, industrial communication, and other specialized settings.

My current model is a low-speed educational demonstration, not a complete internet-connected Li-Fi system. Since an LDR responds slowly, a photodiode would be a better choice for faster communication.

My project demonstrates that light is not only a source of illumination but can also be a medium for communication.

Thank you.

മലയാളത്തിൽ ഉത്തരം




What is Li-Fi?

	

Light Fidelity, a technology that uses light to transmit data.

	

പ്രകാശം ഉപയോഗിച്ച് വിവരങ്ങൾ കൈമാറുന്ന സാങ്കേതികവിദ്യ.




What is the role of the LED?

	

It transmits encoded information through light.

	

പ്രകാശത്തിലൂടെ ഡിജിറ്റൽ വിവരങ്ങൾ കൈമാറുന്നു.




What is the role of the LDR?

	

It detects changes in light intensity.

	

പ്രകാശത്തിന്റെ തീവ്രതയിലുള്ള മാറ്റങ്ങൾ തിരിച്ചറിയുന്നു.




Can it work through walls?

	

Ordinary visible light cannot pass directly through opaque walls.

	

സാധാരണ ദൃശ്യപ്രകാശത്തിന് അതാര്യമായ ചുവരുകളിലൂടെ നേരിട്ട് കടന്നുപോകാനാവില്ല.




Why use an LDR?

	

It is inexpensive and easy to use for a basic demonstration.

	

ചെലവ് കുറവാണ്, അടിസ്ഥാന പരീക്ഷണത്തിന് ഉപയോഗിക്കാൻ എളുപ്പമാണ്.




Is this a complete internet system?

	

No. It demonstrates basic data transmission through light.

	

അല്ല. പ്രകാശത്തിലൂടെയുള്ള അടിസ്ഥാന വിവര കൈമാറ്റമാണ് ഇത് കാണിക്കുന്നത്.
